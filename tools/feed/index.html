Lens for OPNsense

A calm front page for your firewall: who is on the network, what they did, and whether everything is all right — one sentence first, the reason for each area, the OPNsense page that fixes it.

Source and documentation Releases

What it shows

  • System — internet, system, interfaces, temperature, updates, services, certificates, and DynDNS, SMART, WireGuard, NetBird, Tailscale where installed, each with its reason and history.
  • Devices — every device with an identity that survives an address change: its traffic, its addresses over time, where it talks, what it looked up.
  • Who's home, Networks with their ports, DNS by device and by service, Events, a weekly report and a wallboard; widgets for OPNsense's own dashboard.
  • Pause a device until a time you choose, with a reason — the only thing Lens ever writes to the firewall, through OPNsense's own models, visible in Firewall: Rules.

Install, as root on the firewall

fetch -o - https://raw.githubusercontent.com/BxnnyG/opnsense-lens/master/tools/install.sh | sh

After that, updates arrive with System: Firmware: Updates like any other package.

Other things the script does

| sh -s updateupdate now
| sh -s uninstallremove the package and the feed, keep the data in /var/db/lens
| sh -s uninstall --purgethe same, and delete the data
| sh -s statuswhat is installed, and when the collector last ran

What the feed is

A pkg repository signed with an RSA key. Its public half is in the repository, and the install script fetches it from there, not from this page: a key served beside the packages it vouches for would vouch for nothing.
